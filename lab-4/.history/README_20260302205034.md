## Project Overview

**Fishtopia** is a 2D pixel-art fishing simulation game set on a secluded island inhabited by humanoid cat citizens called the Kittezens.

The player controls Finn, who travels to the island after inheriting their grandfather’s fishing business. Shortly after arriving, a storm wrecks Finn’s boat, leaving them stranded. To earn enough money to repair it, Finn must take over the fishing shop, catch fish, manage inventory, and support the island community.

As the story unfolds, Finn begins to learn more about their grandfather’s past and starts to question whether leaving the island is actually the goal anymore.

I also want to support our workflow by:
